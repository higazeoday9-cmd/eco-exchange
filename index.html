Welcome to Eco Exchange

Eco Exchange is a non-profit platform designed for the exchange of second-hand products between users. We aim to promote sustainability, reduce waste, and maximize the lifecycle of everyday items. Instead of monetary transactions, we use Eco Points to facilitate exchanges, creating a fair and sustainable community-driven marketplace.

what is eco exchange ?

Eco Exchange is a non-profit platform dedicated to promoting sustainability and waste reduction. By exchanging products and using Eco Points, our community maximizes the lifecycle of everyday items.

How does it Work?

  1. Upload your unused items to the platform.
  2. Receive Eco Points based on the value of your items.
  3. Use Eco Points to get products from other users.
